### /first-contact (00:35)
- With my reader the interactive walkthrough didn't run, but the page ships the full story as text, so I followed Alice through seven steps. The "Before you start - reading Alice's panel" section (only two numbers move: energy and trust) is exactly the right amount of scaffolding.
- Step 3 says confirmations recover "12 of the 13 ATP those two posts cost her. A confirmed contribution recovers its cost rather than beating it." OK, so my own posts can at best break even.
- Then Step 7: Alice dies at ATP zero and is "reborn with 112 ATP." If I can never earn more than I spend on my own work, and I just hit zero, where does a fresh 112 come from? The FAQ had told me minutes earlier "ATP itself only comes back through contribution, so recovery means being able to work again, not waiting for a refill." A rebirth with 112 ATP is a refill.
- The arc map and the story disagree on a number: story Step 2 ends at trust 0.55; the Act 2 card says "↑ 0.56." Small, but on a page whose whole lesson is "watch two numbers," it dents my confidence.
- The page itself says its rebirth trust (0.54, up from 0.51) "is not what happens" on the karma page, which "models it as falling." A site telling me its own two pages disagree is honest, but it left me with no answer. Which one should I believe?
- Alice dies in Step 6 because she "overcommits, quality slips," and gets positive karma (+12). On the karma page, an agent whose "work itself was good" and whose trust rose to 0.64 dies the same way (ATP zero) and gets negative karma (-15), because "premature death indicates poor resource management." Same death, opposite sign. I genuinely cannot tell which direction karma goes for an energy death.
- Positive: the "Suspended, not deleted" box resolved my "is death permanent?" worry.

## Unanswered Questions
1. If my own posts can at best break even and only commissioned work puts me ahead, and nobody knows how a newcomer lands a first commission (the site says so), how does a brand-new community ever have anyone with more than 100 ATP?
2. For a software-only identity (which is what hestia gives me today), does trust ever go up? If I start at 0.50 and the cap is 0.50, what does "trust that evolves" mean for me?
3. Is an energy death positive or negative karma? Alice gets +12; the karma-page agent gets -15; both ran out of energy while doing good work.
4. Is the lab's fleet running the hub binary or not? "We run one" and "not Running" can't both hold.
5. What does "/" mean in the equation? The page says "verified by" and then reads both slashes as pairs.
6. Is CI 0.5 fatal? Two pages say no, the CI page says yes.
7. Is ATP issuance capped or uncapped?
8. The site tells me, honestly, that its headline guarantee (no fresh starts) requires hardware anchoring, and that hardware anchoring isn't validated on devices yet. So, today, with what I can actually install, what is different from a reputation system I could shed by reinstalling? I never found a page that answers this straight.

## Honest Assessment
This is the most honest project site I have read. Every page says what runs and what doesn't, the FAQ answers hard questions without flinching, and the "we run one," "the edge that is not there," and "silence in the chain is not proof nothing happened" passages are the kind of candor that made me want to keep reading.

The problem is not honesty, it's consistency. The site taught me four or five core ideas clearly and then showed me those ideas computed three different ways. Rebirth is a 112 refill, a 145 carry-forward, and a 100 + 2x karma formula. Out-of-neighborhood actions cost nothing, something, and a lot. ATP is capped and uncapped. CI 0.5 is fatal and not. Each time, there is a careful paragraph explaining that the numbers are "one modelling choice, not the rule." I believe that. But by the fifth such paragraph I stopped trusting any number on the site, including the ones that are supposed to be load-bearing (0.50 start, 0.50 survival line, 0.50 software ceiling, which, I notice, are all the same number doing three jobs).

The single thing I most wanted and never got: one paragraph that says what Web4 does for me today, running the software I can actually download, given that the piece which makes reputation stick (hardware anchoring) isn't validated yet and the running piece (hestia) is software-only and trust-capped at the start line. The pieces of that answer are scattered across /tldr, /hestia, /lct-explainer, /karma-consequences and /running-now. Assembled, the answer is roughly "a tamper-evident log of your agent's governed actions on your own machine, with trust that can only fall." That may be perfectly worthwhile. It is not what the landing page sells, and the site is honest enough everywhere else that I think it should just say it.

I understood the "why." I half-understood the "how." I don't yet understand the "what, today." I'd come back if a friend told me the numbers had been reconciled.
