But if I'm suspended at 0 ATP and "can't act," how do I contribute? And other pages (below) describe a refill on rebirth.

## Honest Assessment
The first 20 minutes were genuinely good. /tldr and the problem half of /why-web4 explained a real problem in plain language, and the site's habit of labelling what is and isn't real (the badges, "Running only counts the lab's own use," the measured safety-gate bypass on /what-could-go-wrong) made me trust the authors more than most project sites.

Trouble started when I did what the site invites me to do: take the worked numbers seriously. The site gives me numbers everywhere, and once I started checking them across pages they didn't hold up. Rebirth has three different arithmetic models. The quality ramp is described two ways in one page, and the example fits neither. ATP supply is "capped" in one FAQ and "no scarcity cap" in the economics page. The forum example hides posts from strangers; the FAQ and MRH page say nothing is hidden. Each page often admits "this is unsettled," which is fair, but when the examples disagree, I can't tell whether the disagreement *is* the unsettledness or just an editing gap.

The biggest thing I found, I had to assemble myself: the only piece marked Running is software-only, and software-only identities are capped at exactly the starting trust and survival line. So the whole "build trust, compound good behavior" story I spent an hour on cannot happen on anything that runs today, and a single bad review on a hestia identity puts it into restricted mode with no headroom. That might be totally intended (hardbound is where growth lives), but the site never says it in one place, and it changes how I'd read /hestia's "Running" badge.

Net: I came away understanding the *idea* well, the *status* well, and the *mechanics* poorly, mostly because the mechanics are illustrated with numbers that contradict each other.
