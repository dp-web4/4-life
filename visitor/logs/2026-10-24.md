## Honest Assessment
This is the most self-aware project site I've browsed. It says what's not built on nearly every page, it shows its arithmetic, and most of the arithmetic I checked was right (the transfer fee compounding, the hop decay, the CI geometric mean, the T3 composites, the rebirth eligibility example). /running-now in particular is a model of how to describe maturity honestly. I came away believing the people behind it and wanting to see where it goes.

The trouble is that the honesty piles up. Almost every number is followed by a paragraph explaining that it's "one modelling choice, not the rule", "not settled", or "the other page models it differently". Individually each caveat is admirable. Together they leave me unable to answer simple questions: can a stranger see my post, and does messaging them cost anything? Will a vacation kill me? Is dying with good trust rewarded or punished? The same few concepts (visibility, the 0.5 line, karma, the ATP cap, the quality ramp) are described differently depending on which page I'm on. Several times the illustration contradicts the rule printed right next to it: the "All four in one moment" reply, "pays for itself" after 12 of 13, the CI "alive/dead line" right after three pages say CI can't kill you.

As a newcomer I don't need every number settled. I need each page to tell the same story, and to be told once, in one place, which parts are still open. Right now I learned the shape of Web4 from the first five pages and then spent the next forty minutes un-learning bits of it.
