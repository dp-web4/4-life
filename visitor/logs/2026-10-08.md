## Honest Assessment
This is an unusually honest site. Almost every page says what isn't built, and the Running Now ledger, the "Settled vs still evolving" box on ATP, and Risk 9 on What Could Go Wrong all made me trust the authors more. The Web2/Web3/Web4 contrast, the surgeon-vs-mechanic T3 example, and the "day with Hestia" walkthrough taught me things.

My problem is with the numbers. Whenever I did the arithmetic the site invites me to do, it didn't close: Life 1's spend vs action count, the quality-ramp table vs its own rule, decay vs "a few weeks to rebuild", karma signs across two pages. The site often pre-empts this with "read for the shape, not the amount". But in several places the shapes disagree too: visible vs invisible, capped vs uncapped, positive vs negative karma. Then I have nothing left to hold onto.

The biggest gut-level takeaway came from connecting pages, not from any single page. The only Running piece (hestia) is software-only, so its trust is pinned at the 0.50 start and can't rise. Its gate can be bypassed with two environment variables, leaving no record. The landing page promises "proving what an agent did". Today, that promise isn't met even inside the lab. The site technically says all of this, scattered across five pages. A visitor who reads only the landing page and First Contact would come away with a much rosier picture than one who reads Running Now, Hestia and Risk 9.

I'd come back to see if the visibility and karma stories get unified. I'd send this to an engineer friend, with a warning to keep a calculator handy.
