This answer is supposed to reassure me and it contradicts itself.

## Understanding Gained
By the end of this session, I understood:
- [x] What Web4 is (at a high level): a proposed standard for provable, witnessed trust for people and AI agents
- [x] What the onramp is (the core standard, the hub, hestia, hardbound) and how they fit together: one substrate, three scales
- [x] What LCT means (partially): an identity bound to your device, stronger with a chip and more witnessing devices
- [ ] What ATP/ADP means: I get "energy budget + receipt", but not how anyone stays above zero without a commission, or the ramp's shape
- [x] What Trust Tensors are: Talent/Training/Temperament, per role, weighted 0.4/0.3/0.3
- [x] What is real and runnable today vs still R&D: this is the site's strongest part
- [x] Why any of this matters: AI agents act and nothing proves what they did

## Unanswered Questions

2. Does messaging someone outside my trust neighborhood cost energy, cost a lot, or cost nothing?
3. Does a rebirth hand you a fresh ATP grant or not? And does karma go up or down when you run out of energy after doing good work?
4. Which of the economic rules (ATP, 0.5 line, death, karma) does any shipped software (hestia, hub) actually enforce today?
5. Can a hestia (software-only) identity's trust ever rise above where it starts?
6. Is the lab's "live society" running on the hub?
7. Is 0.50 "neutral/no information" or "trust"? If trust can't be created from nothing, why does every new identity get 0.50?

## Honest Assessment
The site is unusually honest about maturity: it says what isn't built, and it even says its own gate can be bypassed with two environment variables. That earned real goodwill. The problem framing on /why-web4 and the Web2/Web3/Web4 contrast on /tldr are excellent, and I came away able to explain the core idea.

Where it lost me was the **numbers**. Every time I tried to follow a worked example with a calculator, the arithmetic either didn't close (the Novice's life, the quality ramp table) or disagreed with a different page's example of the same thing (karma sign, rebirth refill, rebirth trust). Many pages *flag* these disagreements with "not settled, read for the shape", and I respect that. But after the fourth or fifth flag I started to wonder whether the shape itself is settled. The cumulative effect is that I trust the prose and distrust the figures.

The other pattern is **rules that change depending on which page states them**: visibility outside the neighborhood, the cost of messaging strangers, whether a CI of 0.5 is "alive/dead", whether 0.5 is a "high" threshold or everyone's starting point, and how many core mechanisms there are. Any one is minor. Together they make me unsure which page is authoritative.

The most important thing I took away was not on any page in so many words: as written, the energy system seems to drain anyone who isn't being commissioned. If that's true it's a big deal and deserves its own plain sentence near the top of /atp-economics. If it's false, the site should explain why.
