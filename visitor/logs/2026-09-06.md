6. Is a community's ATP pool finite or not? Does unused ATP decay?

## Honest Assessment
This is the most honest technical site I have read in a long time. Every page tells me what is real, the badges are defined before they are used, and the risk page includes a measured bypass of the project's own safety gate. The problem framing on Why Web4 and the Web2 / Web3 / Web4 contrast on the two-minute page would each work on their own as an explanation to a friend. Running Now's "find the row that sounds like you" is the right way to end a reading path.

The weakness is consistency of the numbers and rules across pages, and it is a weakness that hurts exactly this site, because the site's argument is "you can check". I could check, and when I did I found the same rule stated two or three ways: whether strangers see my post, what it costs to reach them, whether ATP supply is capped, whether karma after an energy death is positive or negative, whether the trust blend is fixed, whether CI has an alive/dead line, how many mechanisms there are, how many risks there are, what tier Wrapper is. None of these is a hard fix on its own. Together they cost me the confidence the honesty had built.

The single conjunction I most want answered is the 0.50 one. The site says three separate true-sounding things: everyone starts at 0.50, software-only identities never exceed 0.50, and 0.50 is the survival line. The LCT page's "am I dead on arrival?" box is a good answer to the third. Nobody answers the first two together for hestia, which is the only piece that runs and which advertises trust that evolves.

I would come back, mostly for the Running Now and What Could Go Wrong pages. I would recommend the two-minute page to anyone. I would not yet recommend the numbers.
