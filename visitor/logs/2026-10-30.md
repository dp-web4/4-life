7. How many fundamental mechanisms are there: four or five?
8. What does a software-only identity "evolving" its trust tensor mean, if 0.50 is both start and ceiling?

## Honest Assessment
This is the most honest explainer site I have read. Every page says what is real and what is not, and the risk page's ninth entry (the running piece's safety gate can be bypassed with two environment variables) is a sentence most projects would never publish. I came away believing the authors.

That honesty has a cost the site has not paid yet: it says "not settled" in a lot of places, and in several of them the two unsettled versions are not just different amounts but different directions. Rebirth trust goes up on one page and down on another. Energy death earns karma on one page and loses it on another. An out-of-neighborhood post is free, normal, and very expensive on three pages. Each time, the site tells me the disagreement exists, which I appreciate, but telling me the pages disagree is not the same as teaching me the rule. By the end I knew the shape of Web4 well and could not have written down a single worked example of its economics that the site itself would not contradict somewhere.

The one thing I would fix first is not any single number. It is the 0.50 story: start, software ceiling, restriction line, death line, and a decaying score, spread across /lct-explainer, /trust-tensor and /hestia, with the only Running piece sitting exactly on it. A reader who is about to install hestia deserves one paragraph that owns that, even if the paragraph ends in "we do not know".

I would come back to read the risk page again. I would not install anything until question 1 has an answer.
