## Understanding Gained
By the end of this session, I understood:
- [x] What Web4 is (at a high level) — a proposed standard where trust is earned by witnessed behavior, aimed at making AI agents' actions provable
- [x] What the onramp is (the core standard, the hub, hestia, hardbound) and how they fit together — substrate + three scales; the "one shape at three scales" framing worked
- [x] What LCT means — Linked Context Token, a device-anchored identity key
- [~] What ATP/ADP means — energy budget, yes; but the earning curve numbers didn't hold together so I don't trust my model of how earning works
- [x] What Trust Tensors are — Talent/Training/Temperament per role; best-explained concept on the site
- [~] What is real and runnable today vs still R&D — mostly yes thanks to /running-now; hub's status is contradictory
- [x] Why any of this matters — the AI-agent accountability pitch on the landing page is convincing

## Honest Assessment
The front door is very good. The landing page and /tldr gave me a clear, honest picture in under five minutes, and /running-now is the most trustworthy "what's real" page I've seen on a project site. The honesty boxes ("settled vs still moving", "the present tense is the model talking") build real goodwill.

The trouble starts when I take the numbers seriously. The site invites that: it gives formulas, tables, worked examples, and tells me to check. When I checked, the pieces that are internally consistent (trust tensor composites, CI multipliers, fee decay) are clean, but the stories that span pages don't close: karma flips sign between two pages for the same kind of death; the payout ramp is described three incompatible ways; Life 1's trust gain is impossible at the page's own prices. Several pages even admit they disagree with each other, which is honest but leaves me holding two models.

The single most confusing thing was realizing the only Running piece (hestia) gives me an identity that starts at its own ceiling, and that ceiling is the survival line. That might be fine for reasons nobody stated, but as a newcomer it reads as "the thing you can run today can't build trust", which undercuts the whole earned-trust pitch.

I'd come back to see if the numbers get reconciled. I'd point a technical friend at the landing page, /tldr, /trust-tensor, and /running-now, and tell them to skip the arithmetic.
