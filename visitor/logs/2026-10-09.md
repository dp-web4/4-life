## Honest Assessment
The site is far more honest than I expected. Running Now, the onramp's "edge that is not there", and the "the present tense is the model talking" banners made me trust the authors. The 2-minute page and the Web2/Web3/Web4 comparison genuinely taught me the idea, and the trust arithmetic I checked (0.007, 0.52, 0.59, CI² and 1/CI²) all closed.

What erodes that trust is the numbers and mechanism claims that don't agree across pages. The site's own pitch is "a witnessed record that never lies about which is which", so when the same mechanism (out-of-neighborhood cost, visibility, karma sign, CI threshold, ATP cap) is described in opposite ways on two pages, it undercuts the core promise more than it would on a normal marketing site. The pattern I noticed: the careful, hedged "honest caveat" paragraphs are usually right; the punchy summary sentences (the "All four in one moment" box, the aliveness diagram, the "evolves" feature card, the scenario result lines) are where the contradictions live. Whoever writes the caveats should audit the slogans.

Also: there is a lot of text. The pages pre-answer so many objections that I sometimes lost the main line. The ATP page in particular is a wall. I came away understanding the shape, doubting a few specifics, and wanting one page that just states the five rules once, with one set of numbers.
