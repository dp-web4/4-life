## Honest Assessment
The site's honesty is its biggest asset. Running Now, What Could Go Wrong and the hestia gate-bypass disclosure made me believe these people more than most project sites. The conceptual spine (Web2 declares / Web3 assumes key = owner / Web4 witnesses) landed in two minutes, and the LCT and Trust Tensor pages are genuinely good teaching with arithmetic that checks out.

What eroded trust was the numbers *between* pages. Each page is internally careful, but when I followed the energy story across pages it fell apart: ATP is capped on one page and uncapped on another; dying of energy exhaustion earns a +12 bonus on one page and a −15 penalty on another and nothing on a third; spam is supposed to be expensive but is the cheapest thing on every price list; the quality table doesn't follow its own rule. For a site whose whole pitch is "verifiable, consistent behavior proves you're real", running its own Coherence Index on itself would currently fail. A skeptical reader will notice that irony.

The other sinking moment was hestia: the one thing I could install, badged Running, gives me a software identity pinned at exactly the survival line. That's disclosed, but scattered across three pages; nobody puts the pieces together for me, and the conclusion ("your trust can't go up today") is worse than any single page admits.

/how-it-works is too long for a step on the "start here" path, and /onramp is harder than the concept pages it's supposed to summarize. Fix the cross-page numbers and I'd recommend this without hesitation.
